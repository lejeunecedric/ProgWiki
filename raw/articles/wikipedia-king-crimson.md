King Crimson were an English progressive rock band formed in London in 1968. The band drew heavily from the improvisation of jazz and the structured composition of classical music, and helped pioneer the progressive rock genre with their debut album In the Court of the Crimson King (1969).

Tony Levin played Chapman Stick with King Crimson from 1981 onwards. In 1994 the six-member King Crimson "double trio" included two Stick players: Levin and Trey Gunn. Adrian Belew was the group's guitarist and vocalist in the 1980s and 1990s lineups.
