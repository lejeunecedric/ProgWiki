Lateralus is the third studio album by the American rock band Tool. It was released on May 15, 2001, through Volcano Entertainment. The album debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 chart. The title track, "Lateralus", incorporates the Fibonacci sequence. The lyrics "spiral out" refer to this desire and also to the Fibonacci spiral. Related to this, the song's main theme features successive time signatures 9/8, 8/8, and 7/8.

The band's genre has been labelled alternative metal and progressive metalrogresiva. The prolonged running times of most of Lateralus' thirteen tracks are misleading; the entire album rolls and stomps with suitelike purpose.

Drummer Danny Carey, guitarist Adam Jones, bassist Justin Chancellor, vocalist Maynard James Keenan played on Lateralus.
