Philip David Charles Collins (born 30 January 1951) is an English singer, drummer, songwriter, record producer, and actor. He is best known as the drummer and later lead vocalist of the rock band Genesis, and for his highly successful career as a solo artist. Between 1982 and 1990, Collins achieved three UK and seven US number-one singles as a solo performer.

Collins became a professional drummer and in May 1970, after responding to a Melody Maker advertisement, joined Genesis as their drummer, shortly after the departure of John Mayhew and before the band recorded their third album, Trespass (1970).

The following year, following the departure of Peter Gabriel, Collins took over as lead vocalist of Genesis from A Trick of the Tail onward. Collins also became a member of the jazz fusion group Brand X.

In 1981, Collins launched his solo career with the release of Face Value, an album heavily influenced by his first marital breakdown and a deep affinity for soul music, and the hugely successful single "In the Air Tonight". This was followed by the multi-platinum albums Hello, I Must Be Going! (1982), No Jacket Required (1985), and ...But Seriously (1989), as well as international hit singles such as "Sussudio" and "Another Day in Paradise".

One of the world's best-selling music artists, Collins has sold an estimated 150 million records worldwide across his solo and group career. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Genesis in 2010.
