The Wall is the eleventh studio album by the English rock band Pink Floyd, released on 30 November 1979. A double album rock opera, it follows the story of 'Pink', a jaded rock star, as he builds a psychological 'wall' of isolation around himself. The Wall topped the US charts for 15 weeks. The concept was born out of an altercation at a concert in Montreal in 1977, which led Roger Waters to articulate his desire to build a wall across the stage between the band and the audience. The Wall is one of the best-known concept albums of all time - with over 30 million copies sold, it is the second-best-selling Pink Floyd album behind The Dark Side of the Moon.
