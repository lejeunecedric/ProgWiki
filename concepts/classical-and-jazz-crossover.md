# Classical and Jazz Crossover

**Classical and jazz crossover** is the generic name for prog's habit of importing the forms, harmony and techniques of Western classical music and of jazz into rock — reshaping them with rock instrumentation, drum set and electric-amplification energy. It is one of the movement's defining conceptual commitments, alongside [[virtuosity|virtuosity]] and the [[suite|suite]].^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]^[raw/articles/jazz-fusion.md]

## The two poles
- **Classical pole**: prog artists transcribe or re-arrange baroque/romantic classics for rock instruments — most famously [[emerson-lake-palmer|ELP]] performing Mussorgsky's *Pictures at an Exhibition* live, and Keith Emerson's treatment of Bach (e.g. on "Knife-Edge", built from Janáček's *Sinfonietta* and a Bach French-Suite allemande) and of the Mussorgsky suite.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]^[raw/articles/keith-emerson.md] The classical forms favoured — the suite, the [[extended-composition|extended composition]], [[odd-time-signatures|odd metre]], fugal counterpoint — plug directly into prog's love of [[extended-composition|extended form]].^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]
- **Jazz pole**: jazz harmony (extended/alterated chords), syncopation, swing and improvisation enter prog through the jazz-fusion side of the movement (see jazz fusion) and through bands like Soft Machine, which grew out of the same 1960s scene; prog's jazz side is also covered under the [[canterbury-scene|Canterbury]] and Frank Zappa-adjacent sub-scenes. ELP's virtuoso ensembles and Hammond-and-Moog textures also leaned on jazz-organ technique.^[raw/articles/keith-emerson.md]^[raw/articles/jazz-fusion.md]

## Manifestation in the [[emerson-lake-palmer|ELP]] cluster
- **[[keith-emerson|Keith Emerson]]** was the archetype of classical-rock fusion: trained classical pianist who turned to jazz-organ (McDuff) then rock, transcribing and staging classical suites (Pictures, the "Knife-Edge" amalgam) with Hammond [[hammond-organ|C-3]] and [[moog-synthesizer|Moog]].^[raw/articles/keith-emerson.md]^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- **[[jethro-tull|Jethro Tull]]** brought the classical *and* folk side: Anderson's suite-writing (*Thick as a Brick*'s single continuous piece) and side-long [[suite|suite]] form.^[raw/articles/jethro-tull.md]^[raw/articles/ian-anderson.md]
