# Odd Time Signatures

**Odd (or complex, asymmetric, irregular, unusual) time signatures** are metres that do not fit the usual simple or compound categories — broadly, any meter that combines both simple and compound beats. A time signature specifies how many note values of a particular type fit into each measure.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-odd-time-signature.md]

## How it manifests in prog
In the Western popular music tradition, unusual time signatures occur mainly in progressive rock, which makes frequent use of them. Well-known examples include the shifting meters of The Beatles' "Strawberry Fields Forever", the quintuple meter of "Within You, Without You", and Radiohead's "Paranoid Android" (which includes 7/8).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-odd-time-signature.md] In jazz, the Dave Brubeck Quartet's "Take Five" (5/4) and "Blue Rondo à la Turk" (9/8, expressed as 2+2+2+3/8) were key precedents.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-odd-time-signature.md]

## Canonical examples
- **[[king-crimson|King Crimson]] — "21st Century Schizoid Man"** (1969): the band's ethos was to make things complicated "just to show off" — Sinfield described playing "in 7/8 or 5/8" as a deliberate device.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-king-crimson.md] The song's main riff is in 4/4 with a 6/8 middle section ("Mirrors"), and its shifting meters were noted by critics ("shifting meters, and nasty sax").^[raw/articles/wikipedia-21st-century-schizoid-man.md]
- **King Crimson — "Frame by Frame"** (1981): the interlocking guitar figures of the Discipline-era "rock gamelan" are built on alternating 7/8 and 4/4 metre.
- **[[yes|Yes]] — "Sound Chaser"** (1974): a hallmark of Yes's complex, multi-sectional writing, in shifting metres.
- **[[tool|Tool]]** (1990s–present): polymeters and shifting metres are central to their progressive-metal sound.

## Relation to power-chord riffing
Odd metres in prog often sit on top of heavy, power-chord riffing — as in "21st Century Schizoid Man", where the unison sax-and-guitar riff (with power chords) is a proto-metal statement.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-21st-century-schizoid-man.md] The combination of riff-driven heaviness with asymmetric metre is a defining prog device that later fed [[progressive-metal|progressive metal]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-21st-century-schizoid-man.md]
