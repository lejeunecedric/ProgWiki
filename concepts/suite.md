# The Suite

A **suite** is a multi-movement musical composition whose parts are linked into one continuous, titled whole — in prog typically a single long track (often filling an entire album side) split into named sections or movements.^[raw/articles/suite-music.md] It is prog's answer to the classical suite (a set of dances or character pieces) reconceived as one unbroken extended rock work.^[raw/articles/suite-music.md]

## How it manifests in prog
- The suite is the bridge between the [[extended-composition|extended composition]] and the [[concept-album|concept album]]: where a concept album ties *separate* songs to a theme, a suite is one composition internally articulated into parts, often with recurring motifs and tempo/key changes that reward repeated listening.^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]
- In classic prog the suite typically occupies a whole album side (≈20 minutes) and is a showpiece of [[virtuosity|virtuosity]], as performers navigate abrupt changes of metre, key and texture within a single piece.^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]

## Canonical examples
- **[[jethro-tull|Jethro Tull]] — *Thick as a Brick* (1972)**: a 43-minute single-track album that is itself one continuous suite (essentially one long song filling the whole disc), marking Tull's definitive turn to extended conception.^[raw/articles/jethro-tull.md]^[raw/articles/ian-anderson.md]
- **[[emerson-lake-palmer|ELP]] — *Tarkus* (1971)**: the title track is a multi-part suite; the whole *Tarkus* record is built suite-style, with named movements.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- **ELP — "Karn Evil 9"** (*Brain Salad Surgery*, 1973): a ~29-minute suite and the longest track ELP recorded.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- **ELP — *Pictures at an Exhibition* (live, 1971)**: their arrangement of Mussorgsky's classically structured suite, played in concert and released live.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- **[[yes|Yes]] — "Close to the Edge"** and **[[genesis|Genesis]] – "Supper's Ready"** are further classic-side suites of the same era, part of prog's wider experimentation with suites and extended compositions.^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]

## Relationship to related concepts
The suite sits alongside the [[extended-composition|extended composition]] concept, differs from an [[odd-time-signatures|odd-metre]], and is the formal engine of the [[concept-album|concept album]] and its storytelling. In ELP and Tull it overlaps the [[classical-and-jazz-crossover|classical/jazz crossover]], since a suite is the classical form prog most readily imports.
