# Emerson, Lake & Palmer (ELP)

**Emerson, Lake & Palmer (ELP)** were an English progressive rock supergroup active 1970–1979 and on several later reunions, formed by keyboardist [[keith-emerson|Keith Emerson]] (previously of the Nice), bassist/vocalist [[greg-lake|Greg Lake]] (formerly of [[king-crimson|King Crimson]]), and drummer [[carl-palmer|Carl Palmer]] (formerly of Atomic Rooster). The trio was one of the defining virtuoso acts of classic prog, known for arranging classical classics for rock instruments and voice. ^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]

## Key facts
- Formed in 1970 as a keyboard-driven power trio; named after the three surnames of its members.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- Signed to Atlantic Records in North America; released *Emerson, Lake & Palmer* (1970) and *Tarkus* (1971), both UK top-five hits.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- *Tarkus* (1971) — concept suite of that name which reached No. 1 in the UK.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- Their live arrangement of Mussorgsky's *[[suite|Pictures at an Exhibition]]* was recorded at Newcastle City Hall on 26 March 1971 and released as an album.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- *Brain Salad Surgery* (1973) featured the 29-minute [[suite|"Karn Evil 9"]] suite, the longest single track they recorded.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- Reunited sporadically after 1979, including a full *Tarkus* live suite in August 2010 on a tour opening for Deep Purple and Dream Theater.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]

## Signature sound
A power trio built on Emerson's theatrical [[hammond-organ|Hammond organ]] (his customised "Tarkus" C-3), [[analogue-synthesizer-texture|Moog modular synthesizer]], and classical reference points, over the driving rock drums of [[carl-palmer|Palmer]] and the bass guitar and baritone vocals of [[greg-lake|Lake]]. ELP foregrounded [[virtuosity]] and [[classical-and-jazz-crossover|classical crossover]], arranging baroque and romantic repertoire — Janáček's *Sinfonietta* and Bach's French Suites (as on "Knife-Edge"), Mussorgsky's *Pictures* — for rock instrumentation.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]^[raw/articles/keith-emerson.md]

## History
Emerson brought his live trio concept of aggressive Hammond showmanship from the Nice; Lake brought his [[king-crimson|King Crimson]] pedigree; Palmer brought the heavy percussion of Atomic Rooster. Their records were noted for elaborate cover art (by painter and graphic designer William Neal on *Tarkus*) and high-glamour mega-tours with heavy staging.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]

## Influence & legacy
ELP are a keystone of [[symphonic-prog]] and of [[virtuosity]] in prog; their classical-rock fusion and solo-keyboard showmanship shaped both prog and later progressive metal. Their two-cannon stage stunts and staged theatricality made them one of the archetypal "arena" prog bands.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
