# Tangerine Dream

**Tangerine Dream** are a German electronic music band founded in 1967 in Berlin by Edgar Froese, considered a pioneering act in electronica and one of the central groups of [[krautrock|krautrock]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tangerine-dream.md]

## Key facts
- Named among the prominent groups associated with the krautrock label, with Neu!, Can, Faust, Kraftwerk, Cluster, Ash Ra Tempel, Popol Vuh, Amon Düül II and Harmonia.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-krautrock.md]
- Used the [[mellotron|Mellotron]] extensively through the 1970s, on *Atem* (1973), *Phaedra* (1974), *Rubycon* (1975), *Stratosfear* (1976) and *Encore* (1977).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-mellotron.md]
- Early adopters of the [[moog-synthesizer|Moog synthesizer]]; the Minimoog was a staple of 1970s prog, used by Yes, [[emerson-lake-palmer|ELP]] and Tangerine Dream.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-moog-synthesizer.md]
- *Phaedra* (1974) was a Top 20 UK hit for [[virgin-records|Virgin Records]], alongside Faust and Gong releases on the young label.^[raw/articles/virgin-records.md]

## Why they matter
Tangerine Dream carried prog's long-form, [[extended-composition|extended-composition]] ethos out of rock instrumentation altogether: sequencer-driven side-long pieces that made the band the bridge between [[krautrock|krautrock]], symphonic ambition and later electronic music.
