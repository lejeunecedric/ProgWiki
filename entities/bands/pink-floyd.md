# Pink Floyd

English rock band formed in **London in 1965** by Syd Barrett (guitar, vocals), Nick Mason (drums), Roger Waters (bass, vocals) and Richard Wright (keyboards, vocals); **David Gilmour** (guitar, vocals) joined in 1967. One of the first British psychedelic groups, Pink Floyd were distinguished by their extended compositions, sonic experiments, philosophical lyrics and elaborate live performances, and became a leading progressive rock band — and eventually one of the best-selling music acts of all time, with sales exceeding 250 million records worldwide. ^[raw/articles/wikipedia-pink-floyd.md]

## Key facts & dates
- **1965** — Founded in London as an early psychedelic outfit under Barrett's songwriting lead.
- **1967** — Debut album *The Piper at the Gates of Dawn*; hit singles "Arnold Layne" and "See Emily Play".
- **1968** — Barrett leaves (deteriorating mental health); *A Saucerful of Secrets*; Gilmour fully established.
- **1971** — *Meddle*, including the side-long "Echoes" (23+ minutes, the entire second LP side).
- **1973** — *The Dark Side of the Moon* — the band's breakthrough concept album and the best-selling album of the 1970s (45+ million copies by 2013; 996 weeks on the US Billboard chart).
- **1975 / 1977 / 1979** — *Wish You Were Here*, *Animals*, and *The Wall*.
- **1996 / 2005** — US Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction; UK Music Hall of Fame induction.

## Signature sound
Pink Floyd's sound pivots on Gilmour's expressive, singing guitar lines and the dense, atmospheric keyboard/synth textures of Wright (and later Roger Waters' lyric-driven concepts). Rather than virtuosic displays, the band built **lush, shimmering walls of sound** — multitracked voices, tape loops and analogue synthesisers (the EMS VCS 3 and Synthi A on *The Dark Side of the Moon*) — supporting extended, philosophical compositions. ^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dark-side-of-the-moon.md]

The transition from cult psychedelia to mainstream scale is epitomised by "Echoes": a side-long piece assembled from disparate musical experiments, written by Wright and Gilmour musically with Waters' lyrics about human communication and empathy. Its commercial pinnacle, *The Dark Side of the Moon*, was conceived as a concept album about the pressures of the band's lifestyle and Barrett's mental health. ^[raw/articles/wikipedia-echoes-song.md]

## Influence
Alongside [[king-crimson]], [[yes]], [[genesis]] and [[van-der-graaf-generator]], Pink Floyd defined the pop-scale reach of progressive rock; their integration of atmospheric synthesiser texture, seamless side-long composition and concept-album unity became a template for [[space-rock]] and modern art-rock. Their analogue-synth glides and shimmering pads are a benchmark example of [[analogue-synthesizer-texture]], and the side-long "Echoes" is a canonical instance of [[extended-composition]].
