# Hatfield and the North

**Hatfield and the North** were an experimental [[canterbury-scene|Canterbury scene]] band active October 1972 to June 1975 (with later reunions). The name came from the A1 road signage north of London ("A1 Hatfield & the North").^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hatfield-and-the-north.md]

## Career

The band grew in mid-1972 out of blues/jazz/rock band Delivery: Pip Pyle (drums, ex-[[gong|Gong]]), Phil Miller (guitar, briefly in [[soft-machine|Matching Mole]]), Steve Miller (Wurlitzer electric piano, ex-[[caravan|Caravan]]), with Richard Sinclair (Caravan) replacing Roy Babbington on bass. Moving away from Delivery's blues idiom, they built pieces on riffs in odd time signatures and protracted melodies in the Canterbury style. After a first record contract with [[virgin-records|Virgin]], Steve Miller was replaced by Dave Sinclair (Hammond organ, ex-Caravan and Matching Mole), then in January 1973 by Dave Stewart (of Egg) — the classic line-up of Miller/Pyle/Sinclair/Stewart, with the female backing trio The Northettes (Amanda Parsons, Barbara Gaskin, Ann Rosenthal).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hatfield-and-the-north.md]

They recorded two studio albums on Virgin: *Hatfield and the North* (1974) and *The Rotters' Club* (1975, UK No. 43). Guest vocalist on early TV appearances was [[robert-wyatt|Robert Wyatt]]. Joint late-1973 shows with Gilgamesh — including a "double quartet" set — were in some ways the prototype for [[national-health|National Health]], which Stewart formed after the split with Gilgamesh's Alan Gowen, with Phil Miller throughout and Pyle joining in 1977.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hatfield-and-the-north.md]

## Reunions

The band reformed for a TV show in March 1990 (with Sophia Domancich on keyboards) and again in 2005–2006 with Alex Maguire, touring Japan and the North American festivals Baja Prog and NEARfest. Pip Pyle died in August 2006 after travelling home from a Hatfield show in Groningen. Archival volumes *Hatwise Choice* and *Hattitude* (2005/2006) compile BBC sessions and live recordings.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hatfield-and-the-north.md]
