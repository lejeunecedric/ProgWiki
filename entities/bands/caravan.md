# Caravan

**Caravan** are an English rock band from the Canterbury area (originally based in Whitstable, Kent), founded in 1968 by former Wilde Flowers members Dave Sinclair, Richard Sinclair, Pye Hastings and Richard Coughlan. Blending psychedelic rock, jazz and classical influences, they are considered a key part of the [[canterbury-scene|Canterbury scene]] of progressive rock acts, though they never achieved the commercial success widely predicted for them early on.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-caravan.md]

## Origins

All four founding members had passed through the Canterbury-based Wilde Flowers, though not simultaneously. Pye Hastings had replaced [[robert-wyatt|Robert Wyatt]] as the group's singer when Wyatt left to form [[soft-machine|Soft Machine]]; after the Wilde Flowers broke up in October 1967, Coughlan, Hastings and the two Sinclairs formed Caravan in 1968. The band rehearsed in a rented Whitstable house and even borrowed Soft Machine's PA while that band toured the US with Jimi Hendrix.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-caravan.md]

## Career

- Signed briefly to Verve Records, whose debut LP *Caravan* (1969) appeared before the label dropped them; then to Decca Records.
- *If I Could Do It All Over Again, I'd Do It All Over You* (1970) brought a Top of the Pops appearance and festival slots alongside [[pink-floyd|Pink Floyd]], [[yes|Yes]] and Soft Machine; the 14-minute "For Richard" showed their jazz-rock side.
- Their most acclaimed album, *In the Land of Grey and Pink* (1971), was followed by Dave Sinclair's departure and a 1972 split.
- Hastings and Coughlan rebuilt the band around viola player Geoffrey Richardson; *For Girls Who Grow Plump in the Night* (1973) and the live *Caravan and the New Symphonia* (1974, with orchestral arrangements) were critical successes.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-caravan.md]
- *Cunning Stunts* (1975) reached the UK top 50; later albums (*Blind Dog at St. Dunstans* 1976, *Better by Far* 1977 on Arista) found the band out of step with the times, and they split in 1978.
- Re-formed several times (1980–85, 1990–92, 1995–present) and remain active as a live band; *The Unauthorized Breakfast Item* (2003) and the fan-funded *Paradise Filter* (2013) followed. Founding drummer Richard Coughlan died on 1 December 2013.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-caravan.md]

## Sound

Caravan's signature is a warm, melodic blend of psychedelic pop tunefulness with jazz-inflected keyboards (Dave Sinclair's organ work) and extended, whimsically titled suites — a gentler, more song-rooted pole of the [[canterbury-scene|Canterbury scene]] than the jazzier [[soft-machine|Soft Machine]]. They were festival peers of [[gentle-giant|Gentle Giant]]-era symphonic prog without fully belonging to it.
