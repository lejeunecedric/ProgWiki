# Dream Theater

**Dream Theater** are an American [[progressive-metal|progressive metal]] band formed in 1985 in Boston, Massachusetts, one of the genre's defining and most commercially successful acts.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-progressive-metal.md]

## Key facts
- Cite [[rush|Rush]] as a key influence, alongside Fates Warning, Queensrÿche, Tool and others.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-progressive-metal.md]
- *Images and Words* (1992) and *Awake* (1994) institutionalised classic progressive metal.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-progressive-metal.md]
- Progressive metal "reached a point of maturity" with Queensrÿche's *Operation: Mindcrime* (1988), Voivod's *Nothingface* (1989) and Dream Theater's *Images and Words*.^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]
- Covered "Larks' Tongues in Aspic, Part Two" by [[king-crimson|King Crimson]] on the special edition of *Black Clouds & Silver Linings*.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-larks-tongues-in-aspic.md]
- On April 3, 2022, "The Alien" won the Grammy Award for Best Metal Performance — the band's first Grammy.^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]
- Derek Sherinian, later of Planet X, is an ex-Dream Theater keyboardist.^[raw/articles/jazz-fusion.md]
- Toured North America in August 2011 on the leg where [[emerson-lake-palmer|ELP]] performed the "Tarkus" suite in full for the first time since 1974.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]

## Why they matter
Dream Theater are the central node between classic prog and metal: virtuosic ensemble playing, [[extended-composition|extended compositions]] and [[odd-time-signatures|odd time signatures]] inherited from [[king-crimson|King Crimson]] and [[rush|Rush]], marketed to a metal audience — see [[prog-rock-vs-progressive-metal]].
