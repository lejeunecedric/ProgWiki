# Tool

**Tool** are an American rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1990, consisting of vocalist Maynard James Keenan, guitarist Adam Jones, drummer Danny Carey, and bassist Justin Chancellor (who replaced founding member Paul D'Amour in 1995).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tool-band.md] Described as a style-transcending act spanning alternative metal, art rock, and [[progressive-metal|progressive metal]], Tool are known for very long, complex releases, visual arts integration, and a message of personal evolution.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tool-band.md]

## Key facts / history
- Formed in Los Angeles, 1990; emerged with a classic heavy metal sound on *Undertow* (1993).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tool-band.md]
- *Ænima* (1996) shifted the band into a dominant position in the alternative metal and progressive metal movement.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tool-band.md]
- *Lateralus* (2001) debuted at No. 1 on the US Billboard 200, selling over 555,000 copies in its first week.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-lateralus.md]
- Won four Grammy Awards, including Best Metal Performance for "Ænema" (1998), "Schism" (2002), and "7empest" (2020).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tool-band.md]
- Fifth studio album *Fear Inoculum* released August 30, 2019.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tool-band.md]

## Signature sound
Tool's music is built on lateral dynamic shifts — jarring cycles of near-silence and heavy, distorted riffing — with long-form, suite-like pieces.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-lateralus.md] The band makes extensive use of [[odd-time-signatures|polymeters and odd metres]]: the title track of *Lateralus* features successive time signatures of 9/8, 8/8, and 7/8.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-lateralus.md] The title track also incorporates the [[concept-album|Fibonacci sequence]] in its rhythm and wordplay, with the lyrics "spiral out" referencing the Fibonacci spiral.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-lateralus.md]

## Key albums
- *Ænima* (1996) — the progressive-influenced breakthrough that landed the band at the head of the alternative metal genre.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tool-band.md]
- *Lateralus* (2001) — a step further toward art rock and progressive rock territory, with long, complex songs and the Fibonacci-inspired title track.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-lateralus.md]
- *10,000 Days* (2006) and *Fear Inoculum* (2019) — later critical and commercial successes.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tool-band.md]

## Influence
Tool are cited as a dominant act in the alternative metal and progressive metal movement and are named among the progressive metal bands who achieved commercial success.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-prog-metal.md] Their influence on modern metal is widely noted, with System of a Down, Deftones, and Korn cited as examples of Tool's "towering influence" on the genre.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-tool-band.md]
