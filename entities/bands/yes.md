# Yes

## Overview

Yes are an English progressive rock band formed in London in 1968, widely regarded as pioneers of [[symphonic-prog|symphonic progressive rock]]. Their most notable members include lead singer [[jon-anderson|Jon Anderson]], bassist [[chris-squire|Chris Squire]], guitarist [[steve-howe|Steve Howe]], drummers Bill Bruford and Alan White, and keyboardists Tony Kaye and [[rick-wakeman|Rick Wakeman]]. The band's discography spans 25 studio albums, with 13.5 million RIAA-certified albums sold in the US and more than 30 million worldwide.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-yes-band.md]

## History

Founded by Anderson, Squire, Bruford, Kaye, and guitarist Peter Banks, Yes began performing a mix of original songs and covers spanning rock, pop, blues, and jazz. A change of direction in 1970 following the replacement of Banks with Howe led to a series of celebrated progressive rock albums, including four consecutive US platinum or multi-platinum sellers: *The Yes Album* (1971); *Fragile* (1971), which included the hit single "Roundabout"; *Close to the Edge* (1972); and the live album *Yessongs* (1973). Subsequent releases, including *Tales from Topographic Oceans* (1973), *Going for the One* (1977), and *Tormato* (1978), also achieved major commercial success.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-yes-band.md]

The classic 1971–72 line-up of Anderson, Squire, Howe, Wakeman and Bruford produced *Fragile* and *Close to the Edge*, the latter released in September 1972 and reaching No. 4 in the UK and No. 3 on the US Billboard 200.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-close-to-the-edge.md] Yes earned a reputation for elaborate stage sets, light displays, and iconic album covers designed by Roger Dean.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-yes-band.md]

## Signature sound

Yes's signature sound is built on interlocking instrumental counterpoint and close vocal harmonies. Squire's melodic, contoured bass lines and Howe's blend of acoustic and electric guitar anchor the arrangements, while Wakeman's classically trained keyboard virtuosity on the [[mellotron|Mellotron]], [[hammond-organ|Hammond organ]] and [[moog-synthesizer|Moog synthesizer]] added orchestral and choral textures.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-yes-band.md] The band is known for long-form pieces and [[extended-composition|extended compositions]] such as the 18-minute title suite of *Close to the Edge*, an ambitious four-movement work with lyrical themes inspired by Hermann Hesse's *Siddhartha*.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-close-to-the-edge.md]

## Key albums

- *The Yes Album* (1971) — first with Howe; "Yours Is No Disgrace", "Starship Trooper", "Clap"
- *Fragile* (1971) — hit single "Roundabout"; solo acoustic piece "Mood for a Day"
- *Close to the Edge* (1972) — three-track peak of the classic era; 18-minute title suite
- *Yessongs* (1973) — the celebrated live double album
- *Tales from Topographic Oceans* (1973) — double [[concept-album|concept album]]

## Influence

Yes are regarded as one of the two great classic UK symphonic progressive rock bands alongside [[genesis|Genesis]], and their virtuosic, optimistic sound influenced later [[neo-prog|neo-prog]] and [[progressive-metal|progressive metal]] acts. The band's most iconic members — Anderson, Squire, Howe, Wakeman — were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2017.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-yes-band.md]
