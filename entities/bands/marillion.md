# Marillion

**Marillion** are an English progressive rock band formed in 1979 in Aylesbury, the most popular and commercially successful band of [[neo-prog|neo-prog]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-neo-prog.md]

## Key facts
- Neo-prog peaked in the mid-1980s with Marillion's *Misplaced Childhood* (1985) topping the UK Albums Chart.^[raw/articles/neo-prog.md]
- Key albums include *Script for a Jester's Tear* and *Misplaced Childhood*, cited alongside *Fugazi* among the famous neo-prog records (with Pallas's *The Sentinel* and IQ's *The Wake*).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-neo-prog.md]
- The only neo-prog band to reach arena status in the UK; they pioneered fan-funded music with *Anoraknophobia*.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-neo-prog.md]
- Signed to a major label in the 1980s neo-prog wave with IQ, Pendragon, Pallas and Twelfth Night; only Marillion and Saga achieved international success.^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]
- Frequently accused of similarity to [[genesis|Genesis]], though they used a different vocal style, incorporated more hard rock elements, and were also heavily influenced by [[camel|Camel]] and [[pink-floyd|Pink Floyd]].^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]
- 2026's Cruise to the Edge festival line-up included Marillion alongside [[steve-hackett|Steve Hackett]] and Big Big Train.^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]
- Camel's music is widely cited as a primary influence on Marillion, Opeth and Steven Wilson.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-camel-band.md]

## Why they matter
Marillion are the hinge between classic prog and its 1980s revival: they proved the [[neo-prog|neo-prog]] audience could sustain arenas (see [[classic-prog-vs-neo-prog]]), and later invented the fan-financed album model now standard across the genre.
