# Rush

**Rush** are a Canadian rock band formed in Toronto in 1968. After a brief early line-up, they settled into the classic power trio of bassist/vocalist Geddy Lee, guitarist Alex Lifeson, and drummer/lyricist Neil Peart (who joined in July 1974), a line-up that remained stable until Peart's retirement in 2015.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-rush-band.md] Rush are known for virtuosic musicianship, complex compositions, and eclectic lyrics drawing on science fiction, fantasy, and philosophy, and are widely recognized as the band that bridged hard rock, English progressive rock, and heavy metal.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-rush-band.md]^[raw/articles/wikipedia-prog-metal.md]

## Key facts / history
- Formed in Toronto, 1968; original line-up of Lifeson, drummer John Rutsey, and bassist Jeff Jones, whom Geddy Lee replaced shortly after formation.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-rush-band.md]
- Neil Peart joined July 1974, replacing Rutsey; the Lifeson–Lee–Peart trio then remained stable until 2015.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-rush-band.md]
- *2112* (1976) was the band's first major commercial success, reaching No. 5 in Canada, after the disappointing sales of *Caress of Steel* and *Fly by Night*.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-2112.md]
- *Moving Pictures* (1981) became the fastest-selling album in the band's catalogue, reaching No. 1 in Canada and No. 3 in both the US and UK.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-moving-pictures.md]
- Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2013.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-rush-band.md]

## Signature sound
Rush began as a blues-based hard rock band influenced by Led Zeppelin, then moved into progressive rock after Peart joined, with influences from [[yes|Yes]] and [[pink-floyd|Pink Floyd]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-rush-band.md] Their sound is anchored by Lee's high-register falsetto and melodic bass (he played a [[rickenbacker-4001-bass|Rickenbacker 4001]] with stereo output on *2112*), Lifeson's guitar, and Peart's drumming and lyrics.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-2112.md] The band is noted for [[odd-time-signatures|odd time signatures]] and [[virtuosity|virtuosity]]: "Tom Sawyer" from *Moving Pictures* features a backbeat in 4/4 with instrumental and closing sections in 7/8.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-moving-pictures.md]

## Key albums
- *2112* (1976) — the 20-minute futuristic science-fiction title track occupies all of side one, a seven-part suite set in the year 2112 where individualism and creativity are outlawed.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-2112.md]
- *Moving Pictures* (1981) — the commercial peak, with "Tom Sawyer", "Limelight", and the instrumental "YYZ".^[raw/articles/wikipedia-moving-pictures.md]

## Influence
Rush are widely recognized as bridging the gap between hard rock, English progressive rock, and pure heavy metal; records such as *2112* showcased technical expertise and complex composition while using a more direct, heavier approach than the established English prog sound.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-prog-metal.md] They are cited as an influence on a wide range of later artists, including [[tool|Tool]], Dream Theater, Metallica, and Soundgarden.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-rush-band.md]
