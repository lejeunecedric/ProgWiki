# Peter Hammill

**Peter Hammill** (born 5 November 1948, London) is an English singer-songwriter, founder, vocalist and principal composer of [[van-der-graaf-generator|Van der Graaf Generator]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-van-der-graaf-generator.md]

## Key facts
- Formed Van der Graaf Generator in 1967 in Manchester with Chris Judge Smith; his intense baritone and dark, existential lyrics made the band one of the heaviest voices of [[progressive-rock|progressive rock]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-van-der-graaf-generator.md]
- Built a parallel solo career from 1971; his 1970s solo albums were released on [[charisma-records|Charisma]], alongside Tony Banks and [[steve-hackett|Steve Hackett]].^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]
- Wrote the English lyrics for the Italian prog group Le Orme.^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]
- Collaborated with [[robert-fripp|Robert Fripp]] on Fripp's 1979-81 solo-period recordings.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]

## Why he matters
Hammill's raw vocal style and willingness to keep the group's sound unpolished set VDGG apart from the symphonic mainstream of [[symphonic-prog|symphonic prog]]; he is a frequent touchstone for later art-rock and avant-prog singers.
