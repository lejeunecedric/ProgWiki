# Roger Waters

**George Roger Waters** (born 6 September 1943, Great Bookham, Surrey) is an English bassist, singer and songwriter, bass player of [[pink-floyd|Pink Floyd]] and the band's principal lyricist and conceptual driver from the early 1970s.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-pink-floyd.md]

## Key facts
- Formed Pink Floyd in London in 1965 with [[syd-barrett|Syd Barrett]], Nick Mason and Richard Wright.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-pink-floyd.md]
- Played a [[rickenbacker-4001-bass|Rickenbacker 4001]] among other basses.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-rickenbacker-4001.md]
- Wrote the lyrics of "Echoes" (1971), whose themes of human communication and empathy sit over music by Richard Wright and David Gilmour.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-echoes-song.md]
- After an altercation at a 1977 Montreal concert he articulated the idea of building a wall between band and audience, which became the concept of *The Wall* (1979) — one of the canonical [[concept-album|concept albums]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-the-wall.md]

## Why he matters
Waters turned Pink Floyd from a psychedelic improv band into the arena of long-form concept works; his writing on *The Wall* and *The Dark Side of the Moon* remains the clearest template of the prog [[concept-album|concept album]] reaching a mass audience.
