# Robert Fripp

**Robert Fripp** (born 16 May 1946, Wimborne Minster, Dorset) is an English guitarist, composer, and producer, best known as the guitarist, founder, and only constant member of [[king-crimson|King Crimson]]. He has worked extensively as a session musician and collaborator with David Bowie, Blondie, Brian Eno, Peter Gabriel, Daryl Hall, the Roches, Talking Heads, and David Sylvian, with a discography spanning more than 700 official releases.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]

## Key facts
- Born 16 May 1946 in Wimborne Minster, Dorset; received a guitar for Christmas at age eleven and "almost immediately" knew it would be his life.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]
- In 1967 joined the Giles brothers' trio, forming Giles, Giles and Fripp; their only album, *The Cheerful Insanity of Giles, Giles and Fripp*, was released in 1968.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]
- Formed the first King Crimson line-up in mid-1968 with Michael Giles, Ian McDonald, Greg Lake, and Peter Sinfield; the debut *In the Court of the Crimson King* (1969) is regarded as one of the most influential albums in progressive rock history.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]
- Ousted Sinfield in 1971 and took de facto leadership of King Crimson, describing his role as "quality control" or "a kind of glue" rather than bandleader.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]
- With Brian Eno recorded *(No Pussyfooting)* (1972) and *Evening Star* (1974), developing the tape-delay system known as "Frippertronics" using two modified Revox A77 reel-to-reel machines.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]
- Retired from music in 1975 to study at J. G. Bennett's International Academy for Continuous Education, drawn to the ideas of G. I. Gurdjieff; returned in 1976 as a session guitarist on Peter Gabriel's debut solo album, touring under the pseudonym "Dusty Rhodes".^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]
- Founded the record label Discipline Global Mobile in 1992 with David Singleton.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]

## Signature sound & innovations
Fripp's compositions often feature unusual asymmetric rhythms influenced by classical and folk traditions. His innovations include Frippertronics (superseded in the 1990s by the digital "Soundscapes" system) and New Standard Tuning.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md] In the 1981 King Crimson line-up he concentrated on complex picked arpeggios, aiming for a "rock gamelan" of interlocking guitar parts with Adrian Belew.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]

## Guitar Craft
In 1985 Fripp founded **Guitar Craft**, a series of guitar and personal-development courses. The first course began on 25 March 1985 at Claymont Court in Charles Town, West Virginia. Guitar Craft popularised **New Standard Tuning** (NST) — C2 G2 D3 A3 E4 G4, low to high — and ergonomic plectrum technique, often on shallow-body Ovation guitars. By 2011, three thousand students had completed the courses; notable "crafties" include Trey Gunn and the California Guitar Trio. Fripp describes his work as founder of Guitar Craft as his "proper work in life".^[raw/articles/wikipedia-guitar-craft.md]

## Influence
Fripp's cross-picking and disciplined, interlocking guitar approach influenced many guitarists, particularly in progressive rock. His work with Eno, Bowie, and Gabriel carried his experimental techniques into a wide sphere of art-rock and ambient music.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]^[raw/articles/wikipedia-guitar-craft.md]
