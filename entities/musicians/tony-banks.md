# Tony Banks

**Anthony George Banks** (born 27 March 1950, East Hoathly, Sussex) is an English keyboardist and founding member of [[genesis|Genesis]], the band's principal composer throughout its progressive era.^[raw/articles/genesis-band.md]

## Key facts
- Met the other founding members at Charterhouse School; co-founded Genesis with [[peter-gabriel|Peter Gabriel]], Anthony Phillips, Mike Rutherford and drummer Chris Stewart.^[raw/articles/genesis-band.md]
- Remained through the band's longest-lasting and most commercially successful line-up with Mike Rutherford and [[phil-collins|Phil Collins]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-genesis-band.md]
- Bought a [[mellotron|Mellotron]] from [[robert-fripp|Robert Fripp]] in 1971 — previously used by [[king-crimson|King Crimson]] — for use with Genesis.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-mellotron.md]
- Counted among the prominent Hammond organists of prog, alongside [[rick-wakeman|Rick Wakeman]], Richard Wright and [[keith-emerson|Keith Emerson]].^[raw/articles/hammond-organ.md]
- Released 1970s solo albums on [[charisma-records|Charisma]].^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]

## Why he matters
Banks is arguably the most influential Genesis member musically: his arrangements (Mellotron choirs, Moog leads, Hammond) defined the band's sound from *Trespass* through *Duke*, and Genesis was the group later neo-prog bands were most often accused of imitating — see [[classic-prog-vs-neo-prog]].
