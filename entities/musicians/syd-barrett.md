# Syd Barrett

**Roger Keith "Syd" Barrett** (6 January 1946 – 7 July 2006) was an English guitarist, singer and songwriter, founding member and original bandleader of [[pink-floyd|Pink Floyd]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-pink-floyd.md]

## Key facts
- Formed Pink Floyd in London in 1965 with Nick Mason, [[roger-waters|Roger Waters]] and Richard Wright.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-pink-floyd.md]
- As guitarist and then-bandleader he drove the free-form improvisations of "Astronomy Domine" and "Interstellar Overdrive" on the 1967 debut *The Piper at the Gates of Dawn* — foundational recordings of [[space-rock|space rock]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-space-rock.md]
- Left the band in 1968 as his mental health deteriorated; the pressures of the band's lifestyle and Barrett's decline are the emotional core of *The Dark Side of the Moon* (1973).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dark-side-of-the-moon.md]
- Recorded solo albums released on [[harvest-records|Harvest]], the same progressive/underground label as Pink Floyd.^[raw/articles/harvest-records.md]

## Why he matters
Barrett is the "lost founder" of British prog: his brief, vivid output links psychedelia to the [[progressive-rock|progressive rock]] boom, and his fate haunts the genre's most famous albums — from *Dark Side* to *Wish You Were Here*.
