# Adrian Belew

**Adrian Belew** (born Robert Steven Beaver, 23 December 1949) is an American guitarist, vocalist and frontman, the guitar-and-voice of [[king-crimson|King Crimson]] in the 1980s and 1990s line-ups.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-king-crimson.md]

## Key facts
- Before Crimson he had been a member of Frank Zappa's and David Bowie's backing bands and a touring member of Talking Heads, also appearing on their 1980 album *Remain in Light*.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]
- Recruited by [[robert-fripp|Robert Fripp]] in 1981 alongside [[bill-bruford|Bill Bruford]] and [[tony-levin|Tony Levin]] to form the band Discipline, which became the reformed [[king-crimson|King Crimson]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]
- Wrote all the lyrics of *Discipline* (1981); the words of "Indiscipline" came from a letter written to him by his then-wife Margaret about one of her paintings.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]
- During the recording of "Thela Hun Ginjeet" he was harassed by a gang and then police in Notting Hill Gate while gathering street sounds with a tape recorder; the incident became part of the recording.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]
- Reunited with Fripp in ProjeKct Six (2006); in 2024 formed BEAT with [[tony-levin|Tony Levin]], Steve Vai and Danny Carey to perform the 1980s Crimson repertoire.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]

## Why he matters
Belew's vocabulary — reversed-guitar solos, animal noises from the fretboard, new-wave angularity over Fripp's discipline — is the signature texture of 1980s Crimson and a bridge between prog and post-punk/indie experimentalism.
