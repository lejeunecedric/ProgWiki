# Peter Gabriel

**Peter Brian Gabriel** (born 13 February 1950) is an English singer-songwriter, musician and human rights activist, best known as the co-founder and original lead vocalist of [[genesis|Genesis]] and for his subsequent solo career. He is one of the most influential frontmen in [[symphonic-prog|progressive rock]]. ^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]

## Key facts
- Co-founded Genesis in 1967 while at Charterhouse School; was the band's lead vocalist and principal lyricist for its first eight years.^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]
- Albums from his tenure — *Nursery Cryme* (1971), *Foxtrot* (1972) and *Selling England by the Pound* (1973) — are considered progressive rock classics.^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]
- Left Genesis in 1975 following the world tour for the [[concept-album|concept album]] *The Lamb Lies Down on Broadway*.^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]
- Solo career launched with four self-titled albums spanning progressive rock, new wave, post-punk and world music, including the hits "Solsbury Hill" (1977), "Games Without Frontiers" (1980), "Biko" (1980) and "Shock the Monkey" (1982).^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]
- *So* (1986) was his best-selling release, transforming him from a cult artist into an international superstar.^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]
- Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame both as a member of Genesis and as a solo artist.^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]

## Signature sound & stagecraft
Gabriel was known for theatrical performances and stage costumes, adopting personae such as "Britannia" and "The Moonlit Knight". His lyrics were symbolic and story-driven — "The Musical Box" on *Nursery Cryme* was his first song to incorporate a story and characters. He also played flute, accordion, tambourine and bass drum in the band.^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]

## History
After leaving Genesis, Gabriel took a brief hiatus before building a solo career that moved from art rock into worldbeat. He co-founded the WOMAD festival in 1982 and Real World Studios in Wiltshire, and became a prominent human rights activist, co-creating the Witness organisation and The Elders.^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]

## Influence & legacy
Gabriel's theatrical frontmanship and symbolic lyricism defined the Genesis sound and influenced generations of [[neo-prog]] singers. His world-music advocacy and digital-distribution pioneering extended his influence well beyond prog.^[raw/articles/peter-gabriel.md]
