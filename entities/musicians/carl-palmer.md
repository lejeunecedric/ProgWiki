# Carl Palmer

**Carl Frederick Kendall Palmer** (born 20 March 1950, Birmingham) is an English drummer and percussionist, a founding member of the Crazy World of Arthur Brown, [[emerson-lake-palmer|Emerson, Lake & Palmer]], and Asia.^[raw/articles/carl-palmer.md]

## Key facts
- Trained with James Blades, Britain's best-known classical percussionist of the twentieth century.^[raw/articles/carl-palmer.md]
- Left the Crazy World of Arthur Brown in 1969 to co-found Atomic Rooster (trio with vocalist/bassist Nick Graham), before joining ELP.^[raw/articles/carl-palmer.md]
- In the 1980s played in Asia (founding member, joining John Wetton and Steve Howe in early 1981); left Asia in 1991 for the ELP reunion.^[raw/articles/carl-palmer.md]
- Has toured with his own bands since 2001 (including Carl Palmer's ELP Legacy, performing ELP repertoire instrumentally).^[raw/articles/carl-palmer.md]

## Signature sound & gear
Palmer's powerful, theatrical rock-percussion style made heavy use of gongs, tambourines, temple blocks, triangle, church bell and other tuned percussion, and he was known for complex double bass drum patterns. A signature kit was built by British Steel from one-half-inch-thick stainless-steel shells, which toured on a rotating platform; he has also used bronze Paiste kits and, from 2022, his own Vater signature drumsticks. His [[virtuosity|virtuoso]] showmanship matched Emerson's on-stage keyboard theatrics.^[raw/articles/carl-palmer.md]

## Influence
Palmer brought a swing-band-plus-classical percussion vocabulary (gongs, bells, temple blocks, tuned percussion) into hard rock, helping define the "power trio plus showmanship" aesthetic of ELP and influencing later prog and progressive-metal drummers with his bombast and precision.^[raw/articles/carl-palmer.md]^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
