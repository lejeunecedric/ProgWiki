# Tony Levin

**Tony Levin** (born 1946, Boston) is an American bassist and [[chapman-stick|Chapman Stick]] player, best known as the bassist of [[king-crimson|King Crimson]] from 1981 onwards and a long-time session and touring player for [[peter-gabriel|Peter Gabriel]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-chapman-stick.md]

## Key facts
- Fripp met Levin while both were working with [[peter-gabriel|Peter Gabriel]]; Levin had played on Fripp's *Exposure* and in the first Gabriel touring band.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-robert-fripp.md]
- Joined the 1981 Discipline-era [[king-crimson|King Crimson]] line-up alongside [[robert-fripp|Robert Fripp]], [[bill-bruford|Bill Bruford]] and [[adrian-belew|Adrian Belew]], playing bass, Stick and backing vocals.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]
- An early and influential Stick user from the mid-1970s: he brought the instrument to Gabriel sessions and tours and featured it throughout his Crimson work; the 1981 album *Discipline*, with his prominent Stick parts, became a reference recording for Stick players.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-chapman-stick.md]
- Co-wrote all the music on *Discipline* (1981) with Fripp, Belew and Bruford.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]
- Rejoined Crimson through the 1980s and 1990s incarnations; Bruford's 1977-80 solo band also featured him on bass.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-bill-bruford.md]
- In 2024 he and Belew formed BEAT with Steve Vai and Tool drummer Danny Carey, performing music from the 1980s Crimson.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]

## Why he matters
Levin is the connective bass voice between classic prog (Gabriel, Fripp) and the modern era — his Stick-and-bass combination defined the punchier, funk-informed texture of 1980s King Crimson and links [[prog-rock-vs-progressive-metal|prog and prog metal]] audiences.
