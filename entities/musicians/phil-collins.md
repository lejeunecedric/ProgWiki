# Phil Collins

**Philip David Charles Collins** (born 30 January 1951) is an English singer, drummer, songwriter, record producer and actor, best known as the drummer and later lead vocalist of [[genesis|Genesis]] and for his highly successful solo career. ^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]

## Key facts
- Joined Genesis as drummer in May 1970, after responding to a *Melody Maker* advertisement, before the band recorded *Trespass* (1970).^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]
- Took over as lead vocalist of Genesis in 1975 following the departure of [[peter-gabriel|Peter Gabriel]], fronting the band from *A Trick of the Tail* onward.^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]
- Also drummed for the jazz-fusion group Brand X.^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]
- Solo career launched with *Face Value* (1981), featuring the hit "In the Air Tonight"; followed by *Hello, I Must Be Going!* (1982), *No Jacket Required* (1985) and *...But Seriously* (1989).^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]
- Between 1982 and 1990 achieved three UK and seven US number-one singles as a solo performer.^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]
- Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Genesis in 2010; an estimated 150 million records sold worldwide.^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]

## Signature sound
Collins's drumming anchored the Gabriel-era Genesis rhythm section, and his distinctive "gated reverb" drum sound became a hallmark of 1980s pop and rock production. As a vocalist he led Genesis's shift from progressive rock into mainstream pop rock.^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]

## History
Collins led Genesis through its most commercially successful era — *Duke* (1980) through *We Can't Dance* (1991) — while building a parallel solo career. He left Genesis in 1996 to focus on solo projects and film music, returning for reunion tours in 2007 and 2021–2022.^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]^[raw/articles/genesis-band.md]

## Influence & legacy
Collins's pop-prog era made Genesis one of the world's best-selling acts and is frequently contrasted with the band's earlier [[symphonic-prog|symphonic]] Gabriel years. His solo work and drum sound shaped 1980s pop production.^[raw/articles/phil-collins.md]
