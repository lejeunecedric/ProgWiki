# Prog Rock Magazines

Print and online magazines that document progressive rock: album reviews, artist
interviews, scene news and retrospectives. They are primary secondary sources for
ingestion into `raw/articles/`.

## Magazines

- **Prog (Prog Magazine)** — UK monthly (Future plc, launched 2009), the current flagship
  print magazine of the genre; new-album reviews, interviews, covers polls.
  https://www.loudersound.com/genres/progressive-rock
- **Classic Rock** — UK monthly (Future plc, launched 1998) covering prog within the wider
  classic-rock field; frequent Yes/Genesis/ELP features. https://www.loudersound.com
- **Exposé** — US quarterly (since 1992) devoted to progressive and experimental music;
  long-form reviews of independent and RIO-adjacent scenes. https://www.expose.org
- **iO Pages** — Dutch magazine (since 1995) focused on prog and symphonic rock, English-language
  website for international readers. https://www.iopages.nl
- **Mojo** — UK monthly with regular prog retrospectives and artist interviews.
  https://www.mojo4music.com
- **It's Psychedelic Baby! Magazine** — Slovenia-rooted webzine (since 2010), long interviews
  and psychedelic/prog retrospectives. https://www.psychedelicbabymag.com

## Use in this wiki

Magazine pieces are good sources for [[symphonic-prog]] scene history, the reception of the
[[concept-album]] format, and artist interviews that support musician pages such as
[[robert-fripp]] or [[rick-wakeman]]. Ingest articles verbatim into `raw/articles/` per SCHEMA.
