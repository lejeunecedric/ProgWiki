# Prog Rock Blogs and Webzines

Independent websites, blogs and webzines covering progressive rock — reviews, scene
news, retrospectives and databases. Frequent ingestion targets for `raw/articles/`.

## Blogs and webzines

- **DPRP (Dutch Progressive Rock Page)** — long-running volunteer webzine (since 1996);
  reviews, interviews, recommended-albums lists. https://www.dprp.net
- **ProgArchives** — community database and review site (since 2001) covering thousands of
  bands and albums by sub-style. https://www.progarchives.com
- **The Progressive Aspect** — UK webzine (since 2010): reviews, interviews, festivals.
  https://theprogressiveaspect.net
- **Progradar** — UK blog (since 2010) with in-depth reviews and artist interviews,
  strong on the modern prog scene. https://progradar.org
- **Something Else!** — US webzine covering classic rock and prog retrospectives.
  https://somethingelsereviews.com
