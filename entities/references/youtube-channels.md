# Prog Rock YouTube Channels

Official and editorial YouTube channels documenting progressive rock: live footage,
official videos, interviews and documentaries. Useful sources for quotes and
performance history (cite URL and channel in `raw/articles/`).

## Channels

- **King Crimson (DGM)** — official channel of Discipline Global Mobile: archival live
  releases and tour films. https://www.youtube.com/@KingCrimson
- **Yes Official** — official channel: classic videos, live clips, remastered tracks.
  https://www.youtube.com/@yesofficial
- **Steve Hackett** — official channel: solo work and Genesis-revisited live footage.
  https://www.youtube.com/@stevehackettofficial
- **The Prog Report** — interview and review channel tied to theprogreport.com.
  https://www.youtube.com/@progreport
- **Prog Magazine** — official channel of Prog Magazine: features, sessions, festival coverage.
  https://www.youtube.com/@progrockmag

## Use in this wiki

Interview videos and filmed performances support musician pages such as [[peter-gabriel]]
and instrument pages such as [[mellotron]], and document the stagecraft behind
[[virtuosity]]. Transcribe or ingest descriptions verbatim into `raw/articles/` per SCHEMA.
