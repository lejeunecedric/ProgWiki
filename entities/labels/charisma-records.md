# Charisma Records

**Charisma Records** (also known as The Famous Charisma Label) was a British record label founded in 1969 by former journalist and manager Tony Stratton Smith. It became one of the most important homes of British [[symphonic-prog|progressive rock]]. ^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]

## Key facts
- Founded 1969 by Tony Stratton Smith, who had previously managed the Nice, the Bonzo Dog Band and [[van-der-graaf-generator|Van der Graaf Generator]].^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]
- First release was the eponymous debut LP by Rare Bird in December 1969, which gave Charisma its first hit single, "Sympathy".^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]
- Most successful acts: [[genesis|Genesis]], [[peter-gabriel|Peter Gabriel]], Julian Lennon and Monty Python.^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]
- Also released material by Lindisfarne, Hawkwind, the Alan Parsons Project, String Driven Thing, Audience and Brand X, plus 1970s solo albums by Peter Hammill, Tony Banks and [[steve-hackett|Steve Hackett]].^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]
- Acquired by [[virgin-records|Virgin Records]] in 1983 and absorbed in 1986.^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]

## Role in progressive rock
Charisma was the UK home of Genesis from 1970, releasing *Trespass*, *Nursery Cryme*, *Foxtrot*, *Selling England by the Pound* and *The Lamb Lies Down on Broadway*, and of Van der Graaf Generator (including *Pawn Hearts*). It also distributed early [[emerson-lake-palmer|Emerson, Lake & Palmer]] and [[king-crimson|King Crimson]] material in the UK, making it a central label of the classic-prog scene.^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]^[raw/articles/genesis-band.md]

## Label design
Charisma's first UK label was a distinctive magenta "pink scroll" design (1969–mid-1972), replaced in 1972 by a logo using John Tenniel's drawing of the Mad Hatter from *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, which made the label instantly recognizable.^[raw/articles/charisma-records.md]
