# Vertigo Records

**Vertigo Records** is a British record label founded in 1969 by Philips/Phonogram as a specialist imprint for progressive rock, hard rock and experimental music, spearheaded by label manager Olav Wyper. ^[raw/articles/vertigo-records.md]

## Founding

Vertigo was launched in 1969 as a competitor to labels such as [[harvest-records|Harvest]] (EMI's prog subsidiary) and Deram (Decca). It was the home to bands from the "cutting edge" of the early-70s British prog-folk-post-psych-early heavy metal circuit. ^[raw/articles/vertigo-records.md]

## The swirl

The label debuted with its iconic "swirl" logo — an optical illusion design conceived by Olav Wyper during a moment of inspiration in a rain-fogged car window, where he sketched expanding circles to evoke a hypnotic, dizzying effect aligned with the label's name and its focus on psychedelic and progressive music. Lavish gatefold sleeves with psychedelic artwork became hallmarks of its early releases. The first releases came with a black-and-white spiral label, replaced with Roger Dean's spaceship design in 1973. ^[raw/articles/vertigo-records.md]

## The prog roster

The debut album was Colosseum's *Valentyne Suite* (1969). Vertigo signed a roster of groundbreaking acts, including:

- Black Sabbath — whose 1970 self-titled debut marked the birth of heavy metal, and *Paranoid* became the label's first UK No.1
- Uriah Heep — *...Very 'Eavy ...Very 'Umble*
- [[gentle-giant|Gentle Giant]] — self-titled debut and *Acquiring the Taste*
- Colosseum, Juicy Lucy, Jade Warrior

^[raw/articles/vertigo-records.md]

## Why it mattered

Vertigo's stylish swirl label and high production values made it one of the most loved and collectible of the labels that built prog. It later expanded to Status Quo, Thin Lizzy, Kraftwerk and Dire Straits (whose *Brothers in Arms* sold over 30 million copies). ^[raw/articles/vertigo-records.md]
