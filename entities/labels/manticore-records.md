# Manticore Records

**Manticore Records** was the record label formed in early 1973 by [[emerson-lake-palmer|ELP]] as the band's own imprint.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]

## Key facts
- Founded in early 1973 by the band, the same period they purchased an abandoned cinema in Fulham, London as their own rehearsal hall.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- *Brain Salad Surgery* (1973) was released on the band's own Manticore label.^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md]
- The 1973 Manticore reissue of the debut [[emerson-lake-palmer|ELP]] album corrected previously uncredited classical borrowings (Bartók's Piano Concerto No. 2 on "The Barbarian", Janáček's Sinfonietta on "Knife-Edge", among others).^[raw/articles/keith-emerson.md]
- The label name persisted in band history: the 1993 box set *The Return of the Manticore* (Victory Music) and the 2010 "Manticore Hall" acoustic tour by Emerson and [[greg-lake|Greg Lake]].^[raw/articles/emerson-lake-palmer.md] ^[raw/articles/greg-lake.md]

## Why it matters
Manticore is a textbook artist-owned label of the prog era — like [[charisma-records|Charisma]] or [[virgin-records|Virgin]], a label run by and for the music — and the vehicle through which ELP controlled their most ambitious record.
