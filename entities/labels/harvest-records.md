# Harvest Records

**Harvest Records** is a British-American record label, originally created by EMI in 1969 to market progressive rock and to compete with Philips' [[vertigo-records|Vertigo]] and Decca's Deram labels and the independent Island label. ^[raw/articles/harvest-records.md]

## Founding

Harvest was created by EMI in 1969, initially under the direction of Malcolm Jones, and distributed in North America by EMI's affiliate Capitol Records. It was EMI's dedicated progressive/underground imprint. ^[raw/articles/harvest-records.md]

## The prog roster

In the 1970s the label primarily released progressive rock by British acts, including:

- [[pink-floyd|Pink Floyd]] — their UK label
- Deep Purple
- Syd Barrett
- Kevin Ayers
- Roy Harper
- Barclay James Harvest
- Be-Bop Deluxe
- Wire

^[raw/articles/harvest-records.md]

## Why it mattered

Harvest gave the British underground and progressive scene a major-label home, most famously carrying Pink Floyd's UK catalogue. As the 1970s drew to a close the focus shifted toward punk and new wave (The Saints, Wire, Thomas Dolby) and new romantic acts (Duran Duran on Harvest America), plus Iron Maiden's first three US albums. ^[raw/articles/harvest-records.md]

## Later history

Much of Harvest's European back catalogue was divested by Universal Music after its takeover of EMI as part of the Parlophone Label Group. Capitol relaunched the label in 2013 with a new roster (TV on the Radio, Death Grips, White Lies, etc.). ^[raw/articles/harvest-records.md]
