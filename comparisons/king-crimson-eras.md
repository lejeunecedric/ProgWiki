# King Crimson Eras

King Crimson's history is a series of distinct incarnations, each with its own line-up, sound, and ethos, all steered by sole constant member [[robert-fripp|Robert Fripp]]. The four most studied eras are the 1969 prototypical debut, the 1971–74 improvisational heavy period, the 1981–84 Discipline-era "rock gamelan", and the mid-1990s "Double Trio".^[raw/articles/wikipedia-king-crimson.md]

## Verdict / synthesis
The eras are less a linear evolution than a series of reinventions around a constant Frippian core of discipline and experimentation. The 1969 band established the template (complexity, [[odd-time-signatures|odd metres]], [[mellotron|Mellotron]] texture); the 1971–74 band pushed it into free improvisation and heaviness; the 1981–84 band replaced improvisation with tightly interlocking, gamelan-like rhythmic discipline; and the Double Trio combined the heaviness of the '70s with the layered textures of the '80s.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-king-crimson.md]^[raw/articles/wikipedia-larks-tongues-in-aspic.md]^[raw/articles/wikipedia-discipline-album.md]
