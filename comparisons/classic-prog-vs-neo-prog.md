# Classic Prog vs Neo-Prog

This comparison contrasts the first wave of 1970s [[symphonic-prog|classic progressive rock]] with the [[neo-prog]] revival that emerged in the UK in the early 1980s (Marillion, IQ, Pendragon, Pallas, Twelfth Night). The central question is whether neo-prog is a copy of the classic template or a distinct original movement. ^[raw/articles/neo-prog.md]

## Comparison table

| Dimension | Classic prog (1970s) | Neo-prog (1980s–90s) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Era & origin | Late 1960s–mid 1970s, UK | Early 1980s, UK |
| Key bands | [[genesis|Genesis]], [[yes|Yes]], [[king-crimson|King Crimson]], [[camel|Camel]], [[van-der-graaf-generator|Van der Graaf Generator]] | Marillion, IQ, Pendragon, Pallas, Twelfth Night |
| Composition | Extended [[suite|suites]], group improvisation, [[odd-time-signatures|odd time signatures]] | Careful composition, individual solos over group improvisation |
| Keyboards | [[mellotron|Mellotron]], [[hammond-organ|Hammond organ]], analogue synths | Digital synthesizers replacing Mellotrons and organs |
| Lyrics | Esoteric, mythological, symbolic | More personal, dramatic, less esoteric |
| Song length | Long, complex pieces | Shorter, more radio-friendly songs |
| Commercial peak | Massive worldwide success in the 1970s | Only Marillion reached arena status; peaked mid-1980s |
| Influences | Classical, jazz, folk | Early Genesis, Camel, Van der Graaf Generator, Pink Floyd, plus funk, hard rock and punk |

## Verdict / synthesis
Neo-prog is best understood as a revival that consciously reworked the classic-prog template rather than a wholly original movement. It drew its main influences from first-wave bands such as early [[genesis|Genesis]] and [[camel|Camel]], and its symphonic tropes — dramatic vocals, melodic guitar solos, keyboard textures — echo the 1970s. Yet it updated the formula with digital synthesizers, more personal lyrics and shorter, more accessible songs, and it never matched the first wave's commercial heights. The genre was also accused of being derivative and lacking innovation.^[raw/articles/neo-prog.md]^[raw/articles/progressive-rock.md]
